# PromoCheck — Product Requirements (English)

Promotion preflight and draft comparison for small ecommerce operators. The user approved proceeding with the demonstrated product direction. They had no personal incident to contribute; examples use public-rule background and explicitly synthetic data. Visual/session defaults were agent proposals reviewed with the technical plan, not purported direct user specifications.

## Core Journey and Success

1. Open the local workbench and choose synthetic data or import CSV, with provenance visible.
2. Preview activities, products, timestamps, exclusivity and cost assumptions. Empty data, missing columns and unreadable timestamps must produce clear errors.
3. Check and inspect source record, rule, explanation and required action. Missing costs cannot create fictional profit.
4. Edit a separate draft, preserve the original, recheck and show resolved, remaining and new findings plus field changes.
5. Compare complete-cost contribution, including all assumptions and excluded costs. It is not net profit or a sales forecast.
6. Record self-declared review notes and export HTML/JSON with originals, draft, checks, changes, assumptions and unresolved items. Review does not erase an original finding.

Success is one specific finding explained, a valid correction, consistent recheck/comparison and a report that reopens offline. Reproducibility does not mean automatic store publication.

## Screens, Layout and Visual Defaults

One browser-page workflow combines configuration checks, draft editing and comparisons, rather than a whole-store BI dashboard. Import/sample controls come first. Findings follow checks; selecting an activity exposes evidence and editing. Comparisons sit near edits and reports follow review. Proposed visual defaults: white background, dark readable text, blue actions, red blocking and amber review states; status also appears as text. Chinese is default with an English switch in preflight, preserving the current session. No specific visual style was supplied by the user.

## Required Behavior and Acceptance

**Input and provenance:** keep source-record references, reject damaged input without silently losing records, label built-in data synthetic, and invalidate old conclusions when input changes. Empty files must not pass; equivalent CSV and sample inputs must agree. Public rules establish background, not a customer incident.

**Configuration:** validate required fields, real dates/order and explicitly exclusive overlaps for the same product. Show both activities, values, rule IDs and reasons for a paired finding. Ordinary overlap and adjacent endpoints must not be misclassified. The exclusive input is an operating assumption, not complete platform stacking semantics. Error cases and clean controls must exist; fixing one issue must not hide another.

**Draft comparison:** edit configuration, price or discount only in the draft and explicitly recheck. Show before/after values, resolved and remaining findings. Edited-but-unchecked state cannot retain a passing conclusion. Price edits do not resolve scheduling conflicts; date edits do not invent cost improvement. Original findings remain in reports.

**Unit contribution:** one product/unit/currency, explicit price, merchant discount, product/fulfilment costs and fees. Blank is unknown, while zero is an explicit value. Explain calculation inputs and exclude taxes, returns, advertising and fixed costs in this original per-unit model. Negative contribution needs review; nonnegative does not mean recommended launch. Fee-on-discounted-revenue is a simplified assumption, not platform settlement. Hand-calculated examples and missing-cost controls verify the boundary.

**Review/export:** notes mean “reviewed with comments,” not platform approval or authenticated identity. Preserve inputs, original findings, edits and current findings. Diagnostic reports may retain unresolved issues. HTML and JSON express the same facts; user markup is rendered as text. Edits invalidate previous review and current-report availability.

## States and Boundaries

Unloaded: no result or default success. Unparseable: located errors, not a passing partial import. Missing costs: configuration checks remain possible, contribution is unavailable. Checked: blocking/review/no-defined-anomaly are distinct, with no official-compliance claim. Edited: pending recheck. Rechecked: changes and remaining issues visible. Reviewed: original findings retained; edits require new review. Business state is in memory and clears on refresh; warn before leaving and encourage export, with no accounts or synchronization.

## Product Decisions and Non-goals

Use evidenced problems, meaningful corrections and reproducible experiences as the core. Do not expand into generic BI for its own sake. Star counts, graphs and test counts are not independent evidence of value or awards. Sources/licenses and contest constraints govern reuse. Store APIs, accounts, collaboration, comprehensive platform discounts, sales forecasting and cross-session sync are deferred. No automatic launch, substitution for platform approval, or claim that simulated contribution is money actually recovered.

Open questions in the initial PRD were resolved by using labeled synthetic examples after the user said there was no personal case, adopting disclosed visual defaults, and keeping the model narrow if it cannot express a real scenario. Initial “not run” status described the planning stage; dated QA reports now record actual implementation checks.

## Campaign Planning Revision — October 2

Open campaign comparison → edit stock, budget and two activities → compare both/A/B/custom/no-participation → inspect dated stock ledger and blockers → edit dates, inbound or quantities for immediate recalculation → save/copy and restore plan JSON. This was authorized by the user; it is not a completed commercial pilot.

Acceptance: 200 opening units; both at full demand is short 140 and has no achievable-contribution result; A contributes 480 CNY, B 640, custom 100/100 contributes 100. Losses, zero spend, missing fields, late inbound, explicit overlapping exclusivity and budget excess are visible. Only feasible candidates and the nonparticipation baseline can be ranked. Demand is assumed; overhead is excluded; no ROI improvement claim. The Chinese comparison adds selectable paired cards, common-scale positive/negative bars, a shared date axis and explicit contribution/spend differences. Infeasible candidates have no contribution bar; missing inputs remove stale charts; the full table can expand and cards link to ledgers.

## Allocation Advice and Risk Revision — October 3

Apply one feasible allocation suggestion and inspect contribution before/after; undo once if needed. Inspect break-even and 100/80/50/0% sales scenarios for a feasible selected plan. Committed fixed costs remain at lower sales. Generate a pending-review TXT record of inputs, fees, stock ledger, risks and allocation changes. Editing or changing selected plan hides the previous report. Missing inputs remove suggestions/risk/reports; infeasible candidates do not expose achievable-income claims.

The English delivery route presents the same model in a focused workflow with English errors, stock movements and decision records. It is an independent in-memory view, not a state-preserving language toggle for the Chinese planner. Plan JSON transfers scenarios; the Chinese-only paired charts are not reproduced in this focused route.
