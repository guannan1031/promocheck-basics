参考路线 / Reference route

This map is a reference and factual recap, not evidence of a completed hands-on code tour.

  1. src/Judge.jsx, apply: clicking “Apply 0 / 160” changes only custom allocation. Budget, physical stock and assumed demand stay unchanged. update invalidates the old record; a one-step undo retains the previous allocation.
  2. src/core/planner.mjs, evaluatePlan: opening stock is physical − occupied − safety − ordinary-sales reserve. Reservations occur at activity start; later inbound does not erase an earlier deficit. Infeasible plans have contribution: null.
  3. src/core/advice.mjs, assessRisk: keep the original fixed spend when sales fall. In the synthetic B160 example, break-even is 89 units; 50% sales contributes −80 CNY and zero sales −800 CNY. Judge.jsx:createReport includes the inputs and results in an English snapshot.

修改从哪里开始 / Where to make changes

  • Assumption defaults: src/core/planner.mjs:examplePlan.
  • Stock and budget semantics: src/core/planner.mjs:evaluatePlan; update tests/planner.test.mjs with an independently hand-calculated example.
  • Candidate allocation search: src/core/advice.mjs:suggestAllocations. This is a finite heuristic, not a general optimizer.
  • Chinese comparison charts: src/PlanComparison.jsx; English focused workflow: src/Judge.jsx. Both consume the same core functions.
  • English constraint messages: src/core/plannerEnglish.mjs:englishMessage.

可复用的方法 / A practice to reuse

把“提升ROI”改成能检查的验收结果：缺货140件必须阻断收益结论；零销量仍保留已投入的800元。先把假设和手算结果写清楚，再检查页面和报告是否一致。

Turn a broad value claim into an observable acceptance criterion. The automated cases in tests/planner.test.mjs and tests/advice.test.mjs, plus the browser evidence in docs/QA-ADVICE.md, verify those boundaries. Software correctness is separate from business validation.
